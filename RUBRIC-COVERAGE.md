# Haunted Harvest — Rubric Coverage

Graduate Team: Monica Sai Chalasani (@mchalasani1) and Thirunavukkarasu Palaniappa (@tpalaniappa1)

## Scene I — The Bone Gate
- Human activity: three trick-or-treaters walk toward the gate.
- Animals: animated bat swarms and a watching black cat.
- Weather: moving clouds, layered drifting fog, moonlight, and lightning.
- Realistic movement: walking bob, gate atmosphere, flicker, fog drift, bat flight.
- Moving vehicle/object: animated hearse crosses the road.
- Halloween theme: giant skeleton guardians, skull moon, pumpkins/bone gate styling, haunted entrance.

## Scene II — The Ossuary Forest
- Human activity: witch flies overhead; skeleton drummer performs as a story character.
- Animals: owl, crow, spider, and bat swarm.
- Weather: lightning, clouds, fog, wind-swept forest atmosphere.
- Realistic movement: witch path, spider drop, drum motion, bubbles, fog and tree motion.
- Moving vehicle/object: witch broom flight plus cauldron bubbles/flying objects.
- Halloween theme: rib arch, witch, cauldron, bone drummer, haunted forest.

## Scene III — Midnight at the Bone Cathedral
- Human activity: two living visitors walk through the graveyard and sweep flashlights while skeletons dance.
- Animals: circling raven and bat swarm.
- Weather: blood moon, lightning, layered fog, wind-blown leaves.
- Realistic movement: visitor walk cycles, moving flashlight beams, floating ghosts, dancing skeletons, raven flight, Titan breathing/jaw movement.
- Moving vehicle/object: animated hearse crosses the foreground; wind-blown debris moves through scene.
- Halloween theme: giant interactive Bone Titan, cathedral, gravestones, ghosts, skeletons, blood moon.

## Technical Requirements
- HTML5 + CSS3 only; no JavaScript.
- One external stylesheet: `haunted-harvest.css`.
- Three `:target`-navigable chapters.
- `:hover`, `:focus-visible`, `:checked`, and `:has()` interactions.
- Multiple purposeful `@keyframes` animations.
- CSS variables use the required Halloween palette.
- Semantic sections/navigation and visible keyboard focus.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
